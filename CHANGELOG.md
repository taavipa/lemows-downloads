# Changelog

## 0.1.0-preview.20261124.3 — 2026-11-24

- Start or continue a career in Rappavere village. Walk between the farmhouse, shop, workshop and village hall. Buy supplies, build the Starter conversion and enter weekly events from the same save.
- Fit tops and trousers separately, choose a face and combine headwear, glasses and hearing protection. Paint the Starter and choose a race number for its rear board.
- The three audio sliders now reflect the sound level you previously heard. If an older profile muted the master volume, you can turn music or effects back up independently.
- Fabricate the engine adapter from a measured drawing. Cut the plate, drill the mounting patterns and centre bore, then install the engine and drive.
- In the workshop the camera moves to the working end of the tool. Follow a scribed cut or weld seam, or hold the spinning bit on a punched hole. Releasing the trigger pauses the work. The completed edge, bead or hole shows your progress.
- The driver plants each boot when walking, lengthens their stride when running and bends their knees on takeoff and landing. Both tractors have seated steering and braking reactions. A moving tractor can knock down a driver who is on foot.
- The farmyard menu has a repaired timber barn, a farmhouse beyond the wheel tracks and Kusti on your painted Starter. A tractor passes behind the fence in Quick Race and the online paddock. Selecting a night race switches on the course floodlights and headlights.
- Choose Low, Medium or High rendering quality under Settings → Video. The live menu and fitting room render at the display’s resolution.
- The stock cap now fits under alternate hats, and hood view keeps the local rider’s sunglasses out of sight during braking.
- Passing engines rise as a rival draws alongside and fade behind you. The Effects slider controls the race mix.
- The existing courses have uneven wheel paths and crowned surfaces. Rain collects in depressions, tyres displace water and the rider and individual tractor panels collect mud.
- Crews keep their race entry and tractor across timed pit handovers. Update all players before joining a race together.
- A visit to your team box now counts as part of the same pit visit. Crossing back into the speed-limited lane during that stop can no longer result in a second speeding citation.
- Pressing the service brake on the factory Starter progressively unloads the drive belt so the rear brake can slow the tractor.
- In Quick Race, an AI teammate can take a stint in either tractor at all three venues. Request the return from pit service.

## 0.1.0-preview.20261124.1

- Choose Rappavere, Kardiranna or Tolmuste for Quick Race. A Class S AI teammate can take a stint at Rappavere.
- Adjust menu music, race music and effects independently. Check fuel and engine temperature inside the speedometer.
- Pit service supports refuelling, tyre replacement and timed repairs. Hosts can set damage severity.

## 0.1.0-preview.20261120.8 — 2026-11-20

- The launcher now checks and installs updates automatically. Select **Play** when it is ready to open the game menu.
- Solo play starts with Kusti and Starter 1846 at Rappavere.
- Steam menu options are deferred to Phase 2. Direct hosting and joining are available from the game menu.
- Rappavere's offline Class S opponents use a trained traffic model that brakes earlier and leaves more room while racing side by side.

## 0.1.0-preview.20261119.7

- Updated the driving menus and Tab race roster to match the Le Mows interface design. Use the same display name in solo and multiplayer from Settings.
- Reworked the Estonian and English starter calls to follow the race countdown. The starter report sounds on Green.
- Tuned the driving mix so the nearby engine stays clear and track ambience returns as you slow down or get off.
- Improved AI clearance checks and added a reversing maneuver for blocked tractors.
- Refined Starter 1846's steering and impact response. Trackside props use their normal collisions without acting as tyre support.
